**Times:** 6:30 AM, 12 PM, 9 PM UK time

- Sleep (duration, quality, stages)
- Heart rate & stress
- Body Battery
- Weight & body composition (from Index Scale)

### Sunday Weekly Report (Automatic)

**Time:** 3 PM UK time every Sunday

Generates comprehensive report:
- Week summary (activity, sleep, health)
- Goal progress tracking
- Insights (what's working well)
- Recommendations (what to improve)
- Body composition trends

Report saved to: `~/clawd/fitness/weekly_report.md`
